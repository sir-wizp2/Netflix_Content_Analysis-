### 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this task was to analyze Netflix's content portfolio to understand the distribution of Movies versus TV Shows, and to categorize the content by genre to identify the platform's primary focus areas. 

### 🗂️ Dataset
The dataset used for this analysis is the **Netflix Movies and TV Shows** dataset (publicly available on Kaggle), containing information on thousands of titles including their type, release year, rating, genre, and country of origin.

### 📊 Key Metrics & Insights
Based on the analysis performed in Excel, the following key observations were made:

*   **Content Mix:** The Netflix library is heavily skewed toward Movies, which account for approximately **69.69%** of the total content portfolio, compared to **30.31%** for TV Shows.
*   **Top Genres:** The platform's content is highly concentrated in specific categories. **Stand-up Comedy**, **Dramas**, and **Documentaries** are among the most prominent genres.
*   **Global Strategy:** There is a significant presence of "International" content within the top categories, highlighting Netflix's strategic push toward global content diversity.

## Task 2: Global Market Performance Analysis

**Objective:** Evaluate Netflix content availability across countries to identify major content markets and expansion opportunities.

### 📊 Visualizations
*   **Top 10 Global Content Markets:** Analyzed the volume of titles per country. The United States dominates with 47.8%, followed by India (15.6%) and the UK (9.4%).
*   **Geographical Distribution:** Created a filled map to visualize content concentration globally.

### 💡 Key Insights & Recommendations
1.  **Market Concentration:** Over 70% of the content originates from just three countries (US, India, UK).
2.  **Expansion Opportunity:** Significant gaps exist in South America and Africa. Netflix should invest in localized content (e.g., Nollywood, Brazilian productions) to capture emerging markets.

## Task 3: Audience Segmentation Analysis

**Objective:** Analyze content ratings to understand audience segments and evaluate Netflix's content targeting strategies across Movies and TV Shows.

### 📊 Visualizations
*   **Overall Audience Ratings:** A bar chart highlighting the dominance of mature content. TV-MA and TV-14 are the most frequent ratings on the platform.
*   **Movies vs. TV Shows Segmentation:** A clustered column chart comparing how ratings differ between movies and TV shows.

### 💡 Key Insights
1.  **Mature Audience Dominance:** The library is heavily targeted at adults. TV-MA (3,205 titles) and TV-14 (2,157 titles) lead by a wide margin.
2.  **Movie vs. TV Trends:** Movies heavily outnumber TV Shows in mature categories (e.g., 2,063 TV-MA movies vs. 1,143 TV-MA shows). However, TV Shows have a strong, targeted presence in the TV-14 category (730 titles), indicating a focus on teen/young-adult serialized content.
3.  **Children's Content Gap:** Family-friendly ratings (G, TV-Y, TV-G) make up a very small fraction of the library.

### 🚀 Strategic Recommendations
*   **Expand Family Content:** Invest in G and TV-Y rated content to capture the family demographic and improve household retention.
*   **Double Down on TV-14:** Continue producing multi-season teen dramas, as this is a highly engaged demographic.
*   **Maintain Adult Movie Dominance:** Continue securing high-budget R-rated and TV-MA movies to retain adult subscribers.
